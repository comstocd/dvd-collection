Bookcase 175 ranks AFI-listed films first (100 Movies, then the genre lists such as 100 Laughs and 100 Thrills), then Metacritic score, with the IMDb rating used only where no Metacritic score exists; one copy per film, best format kept. Genres, subgenres and ratings are from IMDb, and plot summaries are written from IMDb's synopses; other film details come from each title's Wikipedia article or the page linked in its entry. Searching a genre name (for example "comedy") matches that genre only; searching a subgenre (for example "dark comedy") matches that subgenre. Entries marked CHECK are uncertain spine reads or titles with more than one possible version; confirm against the disc.
